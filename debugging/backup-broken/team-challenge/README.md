# Fix the garden together

Open index.html in your browser and garden.js in VS Code. Use simple names such as Moss, Fern and Clover.

The garden has several problems. Its intended behaviour is:

| State | Expected result |
|---|---|
| Start | No creatures; count 0; “The garden is empty.”; cream background. |
| 1–2 creatures | Correct count; “A quiet garden.”; pale green background. |
| 3–5 creatures | Correct count; “The garden is getting lively.”; warm yellow background. |
| 6 or more | Correct count; “It's getting crowded here.”; pink background. |
| Start again | Remove all creatures and restore the starting count, message and background. The next addition must be creature 1. |

Before each edit, agree what you think is wrong and what check would help. One person edits while the others inspect and suggest checks; switch who edits after a repair.

Use the five strategies: check the foundations; read the evidence; narrow down the problem; inspect what actually happens; simplify and compare.

Change one thing at a time. Test the third and sixth additions, reset, and add another creature. Be ready to show one clue that helped your team and how you confirmed its repair.
